Caretaker

You’re the school janitor, it’s night, and the kids are trashing the place.

  • Godot
  • GDScript
  • 2D lighting
Status
Prototype, played on desktop
Genre
Top-down stealth chase, against the clock
Engine
Godot 4.7, GDScript, Compatibility renderer
Controls
WASD and mouse, or a gamepad
History
A remake of a 3D Unity game I made in 2020, now 2D top-down

Back in 2020 I made a 3D game in Unity about a school caretaker chasing kids round a dark school with a torch, and it’s still sitting on an old GitHub account of mine. In November 2026 I started it again in Godot, top-down 2D this time, and kept it small to begin with: WASD, a torch, a dark school and kids that run away from you. It’s basically hide and seek the wrong way round.

The lights are off, groups of kids are sneaking in, and you’ve got a torch and ten minutes to fix everything they wreck. They run from you, so you can’t just tidy up behind them. Hold the torch on one for a couple of seconds and you’ve identified them (“I knew that was you, Jimmy!”), and they go home for good. Fewer kids means less wrecking, so that’s how you get ahead.

The shift

Three groups of two to four kids come in through the school’s two entrances, and the PA tells you which one. Every kid has a rolled leadership and lone-wolf score, so groups follow their bossiest member, and a kid who doesn’t fancy the leader wanders off and starts a group of their own.

Chairs, desks, lockers, bookcases, chalkboards and the teacher’s chair all have a price. Smashing one goes on the damage bill, fixing it takes half back, and any kid you haven’t lit up for a while runs up a pound a second just by being about. The bill is your score.

Doors open by themselves and block the torch when they shut, and if you open one on a room full of kids they hear it and scatter. When a kid walks into a room the lights come on, which gives them away, and you can sneak over and flick the switch back off.

Four caretakers

Radar and Segway came first, then the Detective and the Assistant came over from the old Unity version.

CaretakerPower
RadarA minimap with no kids on it until you find them. Fire a tag shot down the beam to mark a kid for 15 seconds, or ping everyone nearby while it recharges.
SegwayTank controls with the torch bolted on the front, and a dash. The battery recharges from light, but every dash smashes something and adds to the bill.
DetectiveFix a wrecked prop and a trail runs from you to the kid who broke it, straight through the walls.
AssistantCuts to the CCTV in whichever room the PA names. Night vision shows every kid, but you stand still while you watch.

Bringing the old version across

Once the basics were working, I had an agent clone the 2020 repo and go back through its history (the last commit made it look like loads of stuff had been deleted), read the C#, scenes and prefabs, and write up everything it had that this one didn’t. I went through that list, cut a couple of things (kids physically kicking props about, and a much bigger school with 14 classrooms in four named wings), and brought the rest in.

How it’s built

The lighting is real 2D shadows. Walls and furniture cast them from the torch and from the moonlight through the windows, so kids can hide behind desks and bleachers. I didn’t want the school pitch black, so there’s some global light too, and there’s a light tuner in the pause menu that adjusts it all live (so I could mess about with the torch cone and the overall light level until it felt right).

The kids flee along a safety map of the whole school, avoid dead ends, sidestep the beam and duck round corners to break line of sight. They won’t run past you, so you can corner them. It works on mouse or gamepad, whichever you touched last, and the on-screen hints switch to match.

It’s a prototype and there’s no build to play yet.
