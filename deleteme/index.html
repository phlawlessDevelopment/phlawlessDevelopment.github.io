Delete my data

Phlawless Autochess (com.phlawless.phac) · Last updated 25 October 2026

You can delete your Phlawless Autochess account in the game, or ask us to by email. Either way it removes everything we hold that is tied to your Google account.

Option 1: in the game (instant)

  1. Open Phlawless Autochess and tap STORE on the home screen.
  2. If you are not signed in, sign in with the same Google account you used before.
  3. Tap DELETE ACCOUNT, then TAP AGAIN TO DELETE to confirm.

The account and its purchase records are removed from our server straight away. If you have already uninstalled the game, install it again, sign in and follow the same steps.

Option 2: by email

Send a deletion request to phlawless.games@gmail.com. The button below opens a ready-made message.

Email a deletion request

We do not store your email address or name, so we find your record by your Google Play order number (it starts with GPA.). You will find it in the receipt email Google sent when you bought the ad-free version, or in the Play Store under Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history. We will not ask for a password.

We reply within one month, and usually within a few days, once the data is deleted.

What gets deleted

DataWhat happens
Your account on our server (a keyed hash of your Google account ID and the creation time) and your purchase records (Google Play order ID, product, time) Deleted, together.
Progress, run history, crystals, cosmetics and settings These are only on your phone. Uninstall the game, or clear its storage in Android Settings → Apps → Phlawless Autochess → Storage, to remove them.
Arena boards you played These are anonymous (no name, account or device ID), so they cannot be traced to you and are not deleted individually.
Data held by Google: your purchase, sign-in and advertising data Managed by Google, not us. See myaccount.google.com, and your ad settings at myadcenter.google.com.

Things worth knowing

  • Your ad-free purchase. Deleting your account removes our record of it. Google Play still has the purchase, so you may be able to sign in again later and restore it, but we cannot promise that after a deletion, so only delete if you are sure.
  • Signed in but never bought anything? Then all we hold is an anonymous hash and a date, with nothing that points to you, so an email request has nothing to match against. Use Option 1 instead.
  • Signing out is not deleting. SIGN OUT only forgets the sign-in on your phone; the account stays on our server until you delete it.
  • Removing the app’s Google access. You can also disconnect the game in your Google Account under Security → Your connections to third-party apps & services. This does not delete our record, so use one of the options above too.

More detail on what we collect is in the Privacy Policy.
