I’m a Scottish dev and I make games and tools, mostly with coding agents.

That means I write the briefs, make the design calls, test everything on my actual phone or browser, and the agents do most of the typing. The autochess game started in OpenCode in September, mostly on free models to keep the cost down, then moved over to Claude Code in October. Every sprite in it came out of ChatGPT on my $20 plan.

There are three projects here right now. Mediator is a group chat with an AI that steps in when things get heated (Java / Spring Boot). Don’t Dance is a wee arcade game you can play in the browser (Godot). Phlawless Autochess is an autochess game for Android (Godot again, with a small Cloudflare backend). Each one has a page on how it works.

If you want to chat about any of it, email me at phlawless.games@gmail.com.
