Sandpile

An anti-grav racer on a sea of loose sand that you reshape as you ride, with LAN multiplayer you can join from the browser.

  • Godot
  • LAN multiplayer
  • Browser
Play in your browser → How to join a game →
Genre
Arcade racer, sandbox
Players
Solo against AI rivals, or LAN multiplayer (up to 9, plus rivals)
Engine
Godot 4.7, GDScript
Platform
Desktop (hosts), desktop browser (joins or plays solo), keyboard or gamepad

The whole map is a heightfield of loose sand on a hex grid that slumps at its angle of repose. Your craft blows a bowl under itself, cuts a trench at speed and throws up wake berms. You’re also a sandbender: tap jump to heave a ramp up ahead, hold it to surge on a wave, blast a hump of sand along the ground at a rival, or bend up a wall to stop theirs.

Play it in your browser

It opens on a join screen. Press Play solo to ride against AI rivals, or join a friend’s game on your network. It’s about a 40 MB download and needs a desktop browser with a keyboard or gamepad.

Playing together

One person hosts from the desktop game: open the menu with Esc (or Start) and pick Host LAN game. The menu shows the address to share. Everyone else on the same network opens the play page, types their name and the host’s IP, and presses Join. A link like /sandpile/play/?host=192.168.1.20 fills the address in for you.

The first time a browser joins a given host there’s one extra step. This page is HTTPS, so it can only open a secure connection, and the host uses a certificate it made itself. Press Trust host, and in the tab that opens choose Advanced, then Proceed. That tab then shows an error page, which is fine. Close it and press Join. The host needs port 8910 open on its firewall.

The host runs everything: the sand, the AI rivals and every player’s craft. Browsers just send their controls and draw what the host sends back, which is each craft’s state and the patches of sand that changed, thirty times a second.

Controls

Keyboard
W / S thrust and reverse, A / D steer, Space to tap a ramp or hold to surge, Shift to drift, F sand blast, R sand wall, Q / E look, V camera, T reset, Esc menu.
Gamepad
Triggers for thrust and reverse, left stick to steer, A jump, B drift, X blast, Y wall, Start menu.
